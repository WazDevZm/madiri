About me

Curious about life, in every sense.

I'm Madiri Tariro, a Biology undergraduate at The Copperbelt University in Zambia. I'm fascinated by the living world, from the chemistry inside a single cell to the ecosystems that sustain whole communities.

As an aspiring scientist, I'm building a strong foundation in the biological sciences and growing my research skills. I believe careful science and fresh ideas can solve real problems in health, food security and the environment, and I want to help make that happen.

I'm open to research opportunities, internships, mentorship and collaborations with people who love discovery.
